About Me

I'm a Finance major at Florida State University, where I've kept a 4.00 GPA while building out experience across wealth management, equity research, and private equity-style sourcing work. What draws me to finance isn't just the numbers — it's the way a good valuation forces you to actually understand a business: how it makes money, where it's vulnerable, and what it's really worth to someone else.

That's part of why I've been pulled specifically toward investment banking. The work is dense and detail-oriented, but it sits at the center of decisions that reshape entire companies. I like being close to that kind of impact, and I like that the craft — modeling, diligence, storytelling with numbers — is something you can always get sharper at.

Outside of coursework, I've tried to get as much hands-on reps as possible: interning, competing in valuation challenges, and joining a rigorous year-long IB workshop with a small, selective cohort. Each one has taught me something the classroom couldn't.
