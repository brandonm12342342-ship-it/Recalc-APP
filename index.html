01Summary

I'm finishing a finance degree at Florida State with a 4.0, but most of what I've actually learned has come from outside the classroom: wealth management, equity research, a bit of PE-style sourcing work. I got hooked on markets young, just from watching them, and got more specifically interested in banking after a conversation with my cousin, who works in M&A in New York. What stuck with me wasn't the prestige of it. It was how much thinking goes into figuring out what a company is actually worth, and how much rides on getting that right.

Outside classes I try to get as many real reps as I can. That shows up below as internships, competitions, and a workshop I applied to get into. Each one taught me something a lecture couldn't.
